# Executive Summary  
## Streaming Content Strategy Analysis  
**Netflix · Amazon Prime Video · Disney+**

---

### Objective
Compare the content catalogs of three major streaming platforms to understand differences in volume strategy, content mix (Movies vs TV), genre focus, and temporal trends using publicly available data.

### Data
- **17,352 titles** after cleaning  
- Netflix: 6,234 | Amazon Prime: 9,668 | Disney+: 1,450  
- Snapshot period: primarily up to ~2020–2021

### Key Findings

| Dimension              | Netflix                          | Amazon Prime                     | Disney+                          |
|------------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Catalog Size           | Medium                           | Largest                          | Smallest & focused               |
| Movies %               | ~68%                             | ~81%                             | ~73%                             |
| TV Shows %             | ~32% (highest)                   | ~19%                             | ~27%                             |
| Dominant Genres        | Dramas, Comedies, Documentaries  | Drama, Action, Comedy          | Action-Adventure, Animation, Comedy |
| Strategic Posture      | Balanced volume + variety        | Volume / library depth           | Brand & franchise focus          |

### Strategic Takeaways
1. **Amazon Prime** prioritizes breadth — a large movie-heavy library that supports its broader Amazon ecosystem.
2. **Netflix** maintains the healthiest TV-to-Movie balance and a more diversified genre portfolio, consistent with its identity as a pure-play entertainment destination.
3. **Disney+** deliberately keeps a narrower catalog centered on family-friendly, franchise-driven content — quality and brand safety over sheer volume.

### Limitations
- No official “Originals” indicator available in the public datasets.
- No viewership, completion rate, or engagement metrics.
- Catalogs are historical snapshots; current libraries have grown and shifted.

### Recommendation for Further Work
Enrich the analysis with TMDB/IMDb scores, known originals lists, and more recent catalog scrapes (or licensed data) to move from descriptive comparison to predictive content-strategy modeling.
